Hey guys. This is a quick video to talk you through moving some of the tech that is still plugged in and plugging it back in.

Here is a Document Camera or doc cam. It'll typically be plugged in in 2 places, you can follow the cables and see where it's plugged in. This one is the VGA and it's a little different to unplug.

You'll see a peg on either side of the plug, you need to unscrew each, it can be pretty tight, having a screwdriver handy might be a good idea. 

Once each is unscrewed, you will be able to just pull the VGA straight out. If it gets stuck, unscrew a bit more and try again.

Next is the power cable, very simple just follow it all the way to the wall and unplug it.

Then we want to put the arm down into a safer position.

Group your cables in one hand, pick up the whole camera with both hands. and move it wherever you need to put it.

Next is the desk phones. These will typically only be connected with one cable to the wall. track the cable from the phone to the wall. The connector will look kinda boxy and have a little tab on the top. Push down the tab and pull straight out, if i feels stuck, make sure you are pushing hard enough on the tab.

You can then pick up the entire phone and move it with the cable. Be careful not to drop the handset, they can break pretty easily from a medium height.

Once you are done, you can do everything in the reverse order to set it back up.
