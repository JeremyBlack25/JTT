Quick video just showing the 2 methods to quickly put in tickets for help with technology.

The first method is by going to tdx.canyonsdistrict.org, like normal. But instead of hunting through all the different options, you can easily put in a generic ticket by selecting "Service Not Found". You click through the all of these until you get to the ticket submission. When you are here, make a bookmark of this page so you can get to it easier.

second method is to use the search function of TeamDynamix, this can help you find specific topics to make it easier to put in a ticket that you need. Topics are handy to have for a few specific ticket types. These will get routed directly to help desk or the network or AV teams depending on the type of ticket.

The second method is by sending an email. If you send an email to help@canyonsdistrict.org. it will create a generic ticket that's just like the version showed you.  after a minute or two you'll get an email confirming your ticket was created. It will auto-assign your tech you are assigned to, Derrick for the CDO personnel and the tech over the building.
