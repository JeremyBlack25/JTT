This week's Tech Tip is actually for ME. 

A great quote that floats around the IT department is, "customers don't care how much you know, until they know how much you care." With that in mind I wanted to give you all a chance to tell me how you think i'm doing as a technician.

I've used this form a few years ago, and I got some amazing feedback about how I can improve. The one that stuck with me the most was "Jeremy doesn't know/seems too timid to say 'I don't know'. It's okay not not know something and say 'I'm not sure, but I/we can find out together.'" Now I try to make sure to admit my imperfections when appropriate. I try not to know everything, but I hope to know where we can find the answer.

This form is 100% anonymous, i don't even ask what school you are based from. I only want to know how you think i'm doing and where I can improve. It should only take 30 seconds or so to fill out.
