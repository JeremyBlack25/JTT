Hello, and welcome to another Tech Tip!

Today we are going to be talking about Mosyle. No, it is not the latest TikTok Trend or latest pandemic virus. Mosyle is our Mobile Device Manager (MDM) for Apple Products.

We use Mosyle, as MDM suggests, to manage all you devices: Loading Apps, locking down certain functions, rename, and more.

You probably recognize the icon from your iPads. It loads the app as part of the installation. My Tech Tip today is related to that app. 

Did you know that there are apps available for you to download WITHOUT going to the App Store or signing into your Apple ID? Those apps are in the Mosyle Manager app on your iPad.

First, you wanna find your Mosyle School app. You may need to do a search for it by pulling down on your home screen. 

Once you have it open, click the "self-service" button on the bottom right. No need to login.

It'll take a few seconds to load, but once it does, you can see all the apps that are available through Mosyle. You can tap the (Re)Download button and the app should start downloading.

You can also request any apps to be put on there, including paid ones. Paid apps need to be approved by the principal. The process for this is pretty simple: just put together an email with the app name, how much it costs, and the educational purpose behind it. Then send that email to the principal for approval.

Apps also need to be approved on the Learn platform at learn.canyonsdistrict.org. There will be another tech tip about the Learn Platform, so keep an eye out!

Let me know if you have any troubles, questions, or would like a one on one lesson with Mosyle.

As always if you have you need any help with your technology or have any questions, I'm happy to help! Be sure to submit a ticket through one of the official methods: email help@canyonsdistrict.org or by logging into [tdx.canyonsdistrict.org](http://tdx.canyonsdistrict.org).

Thank you, and have a happy week!
