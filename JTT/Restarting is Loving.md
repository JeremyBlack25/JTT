## How to reboot
There's one simple way to reboot, or turn off and on again. Locate the Apple logo in the top left corner of your screen. 
Click on it and navigate almost to the bottom. 
You should see a "Restart...", click on it
A small box will appear asking you to confirm that you want to restart
select "Restart"
Lastly, if you have any unsaved documents, it'll ask if you want to save them before you continue.
Your computer will breifly turn off and back on.
Login with your username and password, and you're good to go!
