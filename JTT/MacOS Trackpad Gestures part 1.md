Today I want to talk about trackpads, also called touchpad or even a touch surface, is the square below your keyboard on a laptop.

You tell a computer what you want it to do, in this case, move your cursor around your screen and select icons. But what if I told you that it can do a lot more than that? A cool trick called 'gestures' can really enhance how you use your laptop.

⚠️ These are Apple-specific tips, so Windows users... don’t come for me. 😉

First: take 3 fingers and swipe away from yourself on the trackpad. You should now see all your apps and windows fly across the screen and organize themselves so you can see EVERYTHING! This is particularly handy if you're like me and hoard windows like it's 2020 and and toilet paper. You can finally find that window that disappeared behind the mess of other pop-ups from skyward and such.

Next take 4 fingers and swipe away from each other (spread them apart) like you're in a spy movie looking at security footage and someone says "enhance!". Now you've just scared away all your windows as they retreat into the boarders of your screen leaving your desktop fully exposed. This is awesome for when you need to click on that document you always access but can never get to because everything is in the way.

Finally, hit the green button in the top left on one of your windows (your email should work just great). Now that it's gone full screen, you can't see your desktop or any of the other windows. No fear, we can get them back WITHOUT clicking the green button again. Simply take 3 fingers and swipe the direction of your other apps. To the left or right. 

Let's do some quick fire ones:
* spread 2 fingers apart on a website to zoom into a specific part. pinch to return to normal.
* on a photo, move two fingers around each other to rotate
* These tips can also mostly be done on a magic touch mouse.

Here's a link to the full list of what you can do: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102482

You're productivity will now quadruple with these new gestures (don't fact check me).

summary:
- **3 fingers up** = View all windows
- **4 fingers spread** = Show desktop
- **3 fingers left/right (in full-screen)** = Switch between apps/desktops
