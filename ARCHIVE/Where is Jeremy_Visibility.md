## Too Long; Didn't Read (TL;DR)
Outside each of my offices is a sign that gives you an idea where i am. My hope is this makes it easier to know if i'm for sure in the building. My email signatures also includes my weekly schedule, Thursdays and Fridays are meeting/flex days. I'm at meetings or i'm at the school that needs some extra attention that week.

---
I cover 3 schools, this shouldn't be a surprise to anyone, but my 3 schools are; Canyon View ES, East Midvale ES, and Oakdale ES. As you can imagine, that can make it tricky to be everywhere i'm needed when it comes time.

In the last few weeks i've made strides to ensure that I have a sign stating if i'm in the building. Each building is a bit different, but all accomplish the same goal (hopefully).

On that same vein, the signature of every email has my scheduled days for my schools. These days (almost) never change; if they do, i try to be transparent about how they change and it's only for that week. Usually it's because one school has a special event on a day i'm not usually there, so I will swap the two days. The other reason could be that we don't have school on Monday so I spend Thursday at Canyon View instead of Monday.

You are always welcome to stop by my office, i try to keep my signs updated about where i am, and any notes about if i'm gone but coming back. If there is something specific you'd like to see on my signs, let me know.

As always if you need any help with your technology or have any questions, I'm happy to help! Be sure to submit a ticket through one of the official methods: email help@canyonsdistrict.org or by logging into [tdx.canyonsdistrict.org](http://tdx.canyonsdistrict.org).

Thank you, and have a happy week!
