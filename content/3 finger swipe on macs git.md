We are ever inching closer to the end of the school year. Fun things are ahead!

Today I want to talk about trackpads. Now you all know i'm not sporty, and a "trackpad" sounds like the thing around a football field (modern day torcher device if you ask me). However, your trackpad, also called touchpad or even a touch surface, is the square below your keyboard on a laptop.

Trackpads are a simple Human Interface Device (HID). You tell the computer what you want it to do, in this case, move your cursor around your screen and select icons. But what if I told you that it can do a lot more than that? A cool trick called 'gestures' can really enhance how you use your laptop.

⚠️ These are Apple-specific tips, so Windows users... don’t come for me. 😉

You probably already know:

- **One finger** moves the cursor.
- **Two fingers** scroll through a page (like that 18-paragraph family history before the jalapeño popper recipe).

Let's take it one step further: take 3 fingers and swipe away from yourself on the trackpad. you should now see all your apps and windows fly across the screen and organize themselves so you can see EVERYTHING! This is particularly handy if you're like me and hoard windows like it's 2020 and there's no more toilet paper. You can find that window that disappeared behind the mess of other pop-ups from skyward and such.

Next take 4 fingers and swipe away from each other (spread them apart) like you're in a spy movie looking at security footage and someone says "enhance!". Now you've just scared away all your windows as they retreat into the boarders of your screen leaving your desktop fully exposed. This is awesome for when you need to click on that document you always access but can never get to because everything is in the way.

Finally, hit the green button in the top left on one of your windows (your email should work just great). Now that it's gone full screen, you can't see your desktop or any of the other windows. No fear, we can get them back WITHOUT clicking the green button again. Simply take 3 fingers and swipe the direction of your other apps. To the left or right. You've now just unlocked 'Desktops'. We can talk more about those in part 2!

You're productivity will now quadruple with these new gestures (don't fact check me).

summary:
- **3 fingers up** = View all windows
- **4 fingers spread** = Show desktop
- **3 fingers left/right (in full-screen)** = Switch between apps/desktops
