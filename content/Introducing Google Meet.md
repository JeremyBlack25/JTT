# Google Meet
Parent Teacher Conferences are tomorrow, and that means chaos, fun and tech (if you use Google Meet). I wanted to take a quick second to walk you through the basics if you need it. To open it, go to https://meet.google.com and make sure you sign in with your **@csddocs.org** account.

Once you are in you will see a button to schedule a new meeting, when you click on that, your're offered 3 options. 
- Create a meeting for later
- start and instant meeting
- schedule in Google Calendar
![](Pictures/Pasted%20image%2020260922094510.png)

### Create a Meeting for later
This opens a pop-up that gives you a link. You can send this to parents or other teachers and coordinate a time. **Be sure to save this link so you can all be on the same meeting**. If you press this button again it will create a brand new link for a different meeting space. You all use the same link to join the meeting.
### start and instant meeting
As the name suggests, it opens your camera straight into a meeting. You then copy the invite link and send it to whomever needs it. (You can also give the meeting a nickname that attendees can search up and join.)
### schedule in Google Calendar
This is the more long term scheduling option of the 3. similar to the 1st, you create a link ahead of time, this time it's done through your Google Calendar (not linked to your Outlook Calendar. You will need to go to https://calendar.google.com to see your meetings again). The meeting link is pre-generated in the calendar event and you can invite any email address to the event. 
PLEASE NOTE: We cannot send emails to outside accounts with our @csddocs, but the event DOES show up on their google calendar. It's recommended to just send them an email or ParentSquare

You both only need to click on the pre-made link when the time comes and it connects you.
![](Pictures/Pasted%20image%2020260922095439%201.png)
The benefit to this is that now on your main Google Meet page, you have a list of upcoming meetings, you click it and it launches the meeting.![](Pictures/Pasted%20image%2020260922095852.png)

Now go fourth and use your newly discovered Google Meet skills!
