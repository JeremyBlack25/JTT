## 💻 System Tip: Instantly Switch Between Active Programs

Ever find yourself needing to quickly jump from an email draft to a reference document, then back to your web browser, all while juggling multiple windows? Hunting for the right window with your mouse can slow you down. This universal operating system shortcut is the fastest way to cycle through all your open applications.

### What it does:

It allows you to instantly switch to the next open application window with a quick key combination, completely bypassing the mouse.

### How to use it:

1. Hold down the primary modifier key (Command).
    
2. Press and release the Tab key (while still holding the modifier key). You will see an overlay pop up showing icons for all your active programs.
    
3. To cycle: While still holding the modifier key, keep tapping the Tab key to move the selection highlight to the application you want.
    

To select: Simply release the modifier key when the desired application is highlighted.
