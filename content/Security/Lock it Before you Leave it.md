We in IT have a tradition & lesson wrapped into one. When we meet up together and someone walks away from their laptop, and mistakenly didn't lock their screen, we change their background. We do all kinds of backgrounds, the crowd favorite is My Little Pony.
![](../Pictures/My%20Little%20Pony.jpg)

## Why do we do this?
1. For fun
it's fun to play a harmless prank on your co-workers sometimes!

2. To teach a lesson! (most importantly)
We are trying to teach each other the importance of locking your device for _Security_. 

## Security
when you walk away from your work or personal laptops, what are you logged into? your google drive, your email, skyward, parentsquare? That is all logged in automatically, no password or identity verification
