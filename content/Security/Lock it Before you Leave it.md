**TL;DR: Lock it or "Pony" it! 🦄** To keep your accounts (Email, Skyward, Bank) safe from prying eyes, always lock your screen before walking away.

- **The Risk:** Anyone can pose as you or access sensitive data if your laptop is open.

## Security
When you walk away from your work or personal laptops, what are you logged into? Your google drive, your email, skyward, ParentSquare, or bank account? That is all logged in automatically, no password or identity verification, easily accessible. Someone can sit down at your computer and do anything with those accounts.

Here's a few to give you a few ideas:
- Change a password so you can't get in.
- Send an email posing as you.
- Send a ParentSquare message to everyone on your contacts
- Access sensitive data about you or your students.

## How to prevent it?
Honestly it's pretty simple, lock your computer. All our modern computers have fingerprint sign in, this makes signing back into your account easier than a button click! Put your finger on the pad and you're in! to lock your computer, just click your power button (DO NOT HOLD IT DOWN, this instead shuts down your whole computer and you have to sign into everything again).
