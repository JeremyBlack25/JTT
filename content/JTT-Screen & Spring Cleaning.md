Screen Cleaning Instructions:
1. Turn off your device. 
	1. Save your work
	2. Apple in the top left corner
	3. "Shut Down"
	4. "Shut Down" again
2. You can clean most spots without using any kind of liquid. Use light, circular motions starting in one corner and work your way across the screen.
3. Shift your laptop around in different light to check if there are more spots that need to be cleaned.
4. If there spots you can't get off, lightly dampen or spray your cloth. DO NOT put liquid directly on your device.
5. Repeat Steps 2-4 until you're confident you've gotten everything you can.


Keyboard Cleaning Instructions:
1. Lightly shake your laptop upside down to shake out any loose dust or crumbs that can come out.
2. Take your cloth and lightly wipe off the spots you can see to start. Avoid your power button so you don't turn your laptop back on.
3. You can push down on your keys, but be sure to not apply too much pressure and that your cloth doesn't catch under a key and rip it off.
	1. You may want to spend some time on one key at a time to make sure that it gets cleaned.
	2. Be sure to get your hard to reach places, like in between the screen and keyboard.
4. If you can't get certain things off, like the screen you can lightly dampen your cloth and use it to get the harder parts.
5. Take your slightly damp cloth and go around your whole laptop, focusing on getting any grime or fingerprints that you may not have noticed.
6. Last step is to clean your power button, be quick since pushing down on it will turn your computer back on.

Using the compressed air can help to keep dust and crumbs out of your keyboard and keep it cleaner for longer, if you use it every so often.
