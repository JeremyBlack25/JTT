### 🔍 Efficiency Tip: Use "Spotlight" as a Calculator & Converter

Ever break your concentration to open the Calculator app for a quick sum, or launch a new browser tab just to check how many ounces are in a cup or convert currency? Switching context like this interrupts your flow and clutters your screen with apps you only need for ten seconds. This built-in search bar is actually a powerful utility tool in disguise.

What it does: It acts as an instant calculator, unit converter, and dictionary overlay. You can solve math equations, convert currency (e.g., USD to EUR), or check measurements without ever opening an application or leaving your current window.

How to use it:

1. Open: Press Command (⌘) + Spacebar to open the Spotlight Search bar.
    
2. Type:
    

- For Math: Just type the equation (e.g., 120 * 4 or 500 / 12). The answer appears immediately in the search result.
    
- For Conversions: Type the conversion you need (e.g., 100 usd in eur or 25 c to f).
    

4. Copy: Highlight the result and press Command + C to copy the answer to your clipboard, then hit Esc to vanish the bar.
