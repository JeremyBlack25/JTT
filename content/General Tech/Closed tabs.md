Ever accidentally close a tab (or a whole window of tabs!) and sigh as you realize you have to go hunting through your history to find it? This simple keyboard shortcut is an absolute lifesaver for all Chrome users.

**What it does:** It immediately reopens the very last tab you closed, bringing it and its history right back to where it was. You can repeatedly press the shortcut to bring back the last 10-15 closed tabs in the order you closed them.
    
How to use it:
1. Make sure Chrome is the active application.
    
2. Use the keyboard shortcut:
    

- On Mac: Command+Shift+T
    
- On Windows: Control+Shift+T
    

Why it's powerful: You can recover an entire closed browser window with one click! If you accidentally close an entire Chrome window with 15 tabs open, pressing this shortcut once will restore the whole window. It's the ultimate 'undo' button for your browsing session.
