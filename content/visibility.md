## 📍 Where’s Your Tech? How to Find Me Each Week

### TL;DR: Where is Jeremy?

- **Check the Door:** New signs at my office tell you if I’m in the building or in a classroom.
- **Check the Signature:** My email signature shows my school rotation (**Mon:** Canyon View | **Tue:** Oakdale | **Wed:** East Midvale).
- **The "Bat-Signal":** If I'm not in my office, the fastest way to get help is a ticket at **tdx.canyonsdistrict.org**.
---

This week’s tip comes directly from your feedback! Someone recently asked: "Can you be more visible when you are in the building, so we know you are here and available? Thank you!!" I hear you! Covering three schools (**Canyon View**, **East Midvale**, and **Oakdale**) means I’m a bit of a moving target. Here is the best way to track me down:

### 1. Check My Office Door

I have placed signs outside my office at each school. These are updated daily to let you know if I’m in the room, in the building, or at another site. (i'm attaching pictures of my signs so you know what to look for)

- **Pro Tip:** If the sign says I'm in the building but not in the office, I'm likely in a classroom helping a staff member and will be back soon!
    
### 2. The "Signature" Schedule

Check the bottom of any email I send. My signature includes my weekly rotation. While Thursdays and Fridays are "flex/meeting" days, my Mon-Wed schedule is almost always:

- **Monday:** Canyon View ES
- **Tuesday:** Oakdale ES
- **Wednesday:** East Midvale ES

### 3. The "Official" Bat-Signal

If you need me urgently and I’m not in my office, the fastest way to get on my radar is a ticket. This ensures that even if I’m driving between schools, your issue is in the queue.

- **Email:** help@canyonsdistrict.org
- **Web:** [tdx.canyonsdistrict.org](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://tdx.canyonsdistrict.org)

---

**I’m here to help!** If there is something specific you’d like to see on my office signs to make them more helpful, please let me know.

Have a great week!
