In a world of electronics and everything needing to connect to everything, it can sometimes be difficult to know when things are broken, when it isn't wifi, just being slow, or some combo!

Well, i wanna give you a few tips and tricks to help you find out. Knowing what's wrong makes it faster to fix the problem.

First thing to find out if it's an internet issue, try just a normal Google search. open a new tab, and i'll just simply search the world "hello". if it pops up with pictures of Adele, i know i'm connected to the internet. This will work on laptops, phones and iPads.

## So internet isn't down, what now?
try a few websites that you know should work, practically, all the time. a few i use:
https://speedtest.uen.net
https://speedtest.net
https://amazon.com
https://youtube.com

If you are having trouble getting to these or other websites to work, but google works fine, then you may need to call me. But if they ARE working, then there's a chance that first site is down or having issues.

## My Apple TV Won't connect
That's not likely a wifi issue, unless you're not on CSD-Auth, more likely the AppleTV (ATV) or your computer needs to be rebooted. Try your computer first, and then reach out if it's still not working. I or the AV team will help reboot it.

## My phone is slow/struggling to connect.
Make sure you're connected to CSD-Auth wifi. We don't have any control over your data service and with these old buildings, they block a lot of that signal.

## I have ... app that won't work when it used to.
Same thing, make sure you are on CSD-Auth Wifi, make sure the app and device is updated fully. Still not working? Reach out and let's figure it out together.

Hopefully these tips help you find the root cause of some of your main issues.
