Many of you have heard me talk about "Powerwashing" a Chromebook. I'm sure that raises an eyebrow or two, so let me explain what I mean and why it's the **BEST thing** you can do to fix a Chromebook issue.

A Powerwash is simply another name for a **Factory Reset**. It erases all data and settings, returning the device to its original factory state—just like it’s brand new.

### **When should you Powerwash?**

If the issue isn’t a physically broken screen or keyboard, a Powerwash fixes it about 90% of the time. It resolves most login issues, weird screen glitches, persistent error messages, and general slowness.

### **How to Powerwash (In under a minute!)**

1. **Sign Out:** Make sure you are on the main login screen.
2. **The Shortcut:** Press **Ctrl + Alt + Shift + R**.
3. **Confirm:** A prompt will ask if you’re sure; click to confirm/restart.
4. **The Reset:** The Chromebook will restart and eventually show a “Welcome!” screen.
5. **Setup:** Click through the prompts until you reach the Wi-Fi selection. Select **“Canyons-Guest”**.
6. **Terms:** Accept the terms and conditions.
7. **Finalize:** It will take a minute to configure. Once you see the "You're ready to go" screen, click **Done**.

### **Why does this work?**

By clearing out the "junk" and resetting the OS, we get the Chromebook back in order in a flash. Since almost everything is stored in the Google Cloud, student data is safe (unless they saved files locally to the Downloads folder, which is discouraged). Once they log back in, their profile downloads automatically and they are right back where they left off!
