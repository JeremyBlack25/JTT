## What is Apple Classroom
It's a program that let's you monitor and manage your students through their iPads. Very similar to Lanschool Air if you're familiar with it. You can view their screen, push out apps and files and even lock down what app they stay on.

It's not a perfect system, and they students may find ways around the app. It's not meant to replace all the things you're already doing, but it should make those other things easier.

Notes before you start:
* only need the app on your staff device (it should already be there, let me know if not)
* You going to want to assign an iPad per student, otherwise it could make it harder to know who's not on task


#### Teacher iPad Setup Instructions
1. Open Apple Classroom on your staff iPad.
	1. You can find it in your "Mosyle School" App under Self-service.
2. Assign yourself a name, this is what your students will see when you make changes to their iPads
3. Create a new class
	1. choose a class name, not important from what I can tell.
	2. description
	3. choose an icon for your class
	4. choose a color
4. Open your new class
5. "invite students"
6. now you are ready to start enrolling your iPads

#### Enrolling iPads
1. On student iPad, navigate to the settings app
2. Underneath Bluetooth, a new icon should appear. Classroom
3. at the bottom, set the "Allow Teacher to:" options to Always
	1. Otherwise it will ask permission from the students when you try to make changes, and likely they'll press 'no'
4. You will notice that your class name will appear at the top of this screen.
5. tap on it
6. You will then be able to name the iPad, you can use a numbering system, or the students' name that will be assigned to that device.
	1. Both are great, use what you prefer
7. You can then put in the 4 digit code on your staff device
8. Press "add"
9. Once they are all in, you can press "add" on your staff device

You're now ready to start using the classes.
* You do NOT need to do the setup every time. The student iPads should remember these settings.
* Over the summer, we factory reset all chromebooks and iPads. So you will need to set it up again at the start of the year.
	* Other than that, i WILL get your permission before I factory reset ipads.


#### Walkthrough
* you can see that it gives a "live" preview of the students' screen.
* From top left to top right
	* launch any app onto their device
		* lock app once open
	* Navigate for them
		* Send Web link stright to their iPad
	* switch view
	* close out of current app and go to home screen
	* lock iPad
	* mute iPads
	* extra options
		* add new students
		* remove students
		* end class
			* gives breakdown of apps the students were on
* tap on a student 
	* all the same options, plus airplay their device and remove them directly
