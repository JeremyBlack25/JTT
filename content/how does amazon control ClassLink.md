I thought i'd take this opportunity to explain how Amazon and Classlink are related and why Amazon going down means that a bunch of other sites don't work either.

# How Amazon relates to Classlink and others
First thing we should talk about is servers. Not the wonderful waiters and waitresses you see when you go out to eat, but a computer server. A server is any kind of computer that serves a remote purpose for users to access. This can be hosting a website like canva.com or a load of other nerdy purposes I won't go into.

Amazon has dozens of buildings filled with lots of computers that companies can go in and rent out to use for servers. This service is called Amazon Web Services (AWS). This is convenient since you don't have to pay the upfront cost of buying your own server and paying to maintain it. Expenses like that add up fast!

Classlink is a company that has paid AWS to use their hardware to host the website and databases involved with the login for us and all the other school districts that use them. It's estimated that AWS hosts about 30% of the internet as of July of this year. The top three being: AWS, Microsoft, and Google. Together they host about 60% of the internet: netflix, Wikipedia, ebay, payment services like Venmo and more.

I think this is fascinating because we don't really think about how the internet works, it just does. And then days like today happen where everything goes down and we start wondering "why".
