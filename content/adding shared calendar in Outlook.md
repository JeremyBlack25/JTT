Calendars can be both helpful and frustrating. Especially if you've ever tried to add one of the shared calendars on Outlook. Luckily it doesn't have to be so annoying! once you know the tricks it becomes a lot less daunting and you see that it's quite easy!

These instructions are for the 'new' outlook that came out a few years ago. If you don't have this version, reach out and I can help you get it set up.

instructions:
1. Open your outlook app (or web outlook in your browser)
2. Navigate to calendars on the left side of the window
3. On the left again, you should see a "+ Add Calendar"
	1. or you can right click (click with 2 fingers) on your email showing your calendars and click "Add Shared Calendar"
4. Then you'll have a search box show up, this is where you can search for the calendar.
	1. remember that we use special characters to help keep all the emails and calendars organized.
	2. for example:
		1. Canyon View's events calendar is: canyonviewES.events@canyonsdistrict.org
		2. room calendars typically have "room-" in front of it and no period
			1. Room-CanyonViewESKiva@canyonsdistrict.org
5. Once you find your calendar, you can click "open" and you'll see it add to your list of calendars on the left bar. 
6. If it gives you an error saying that you don't have permission to view it, let me know and we can take a look at permissions.
