Inventory is a big part of IT and technology in general. When you consider that we have 30,000 students with assigned devices, plus roughly 10,000 staff throughout the district who mostly have devices as well—it's our job to keep track of all the computers and devices that everyone uses, from Chromebooks and laptops to iPads and computer labs. We track ALL of it.

With that being said, I've just received new instructions on how IT leadership wants me to verify inventory for this laptop rotation. I'm not supposed to use a Google Form or other types of data collection. This year, they want me or my assistants to walk around and physically touch and document each laptop. So this completely nullifies the Google Form I've been working on... 😭

### **What now?** 
For the rest of this week, my assistants and I will be walking around the building and tracking every computer we can find. When we swing by, it should only take 30 seconds or so. Here's what we'll do:

1. Write down your name
    
2. Scan your laptop's asset tag
    
3. Write down the Serial Number from your laptop if the tool says we need it
    

Once that's done, you're off my list and I won't bug you again!

_Note: We will only track one computer per person—whichever is your main computer. District policy is that you only have one laptop, and that's all I'm supposed to track._


As always if you need any help with your technology or have any questions, I'm happy to help! Be sure to submit a ticket through one of the official methods.

Thank you, and have a happy week!
