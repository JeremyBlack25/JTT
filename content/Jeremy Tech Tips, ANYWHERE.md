I had a few goals when writing these tips for the first time. 
1. I wanted to share knowledge that can make your lives and jobs easier. 
2. I can't be everywhere at once, and it's so frustrating when you have an issue and it seems like no one is trying to help. If it's something that you can fix, i'm trying to write it all down so you *can* fix it.
3. I don't want anyone to feel awkward to reach out to me. It is LITERALLY my job to help, **you aren't a burden for asking for help!**

All of this leads to my point. **It's not practical to give you a tip that you can never see again.** That's why I found a way to upload all my tech tips to a website/blog that you can easily search and review ANY tech tip i've ever sent. It's even easily searchable with the search bar! Just type in a key word and watch as the tips filter themselves out if they have it or not.

I have nearly 50 articles on all different subjects and I hope they are ALL helpful. Please don't hesitate to reach out for help, or even suggestions of articles.
