You're probably wondering why the title of this article is called "Summer Soup". That's a really good Question! It's because I turn into soup in summer from the heat. The district turns off the heat in the schools over the summer, so things get warm.

The end of the year is here! In just a few short days you all will be on a beach somewhere, cold drink in hand and not a child in sight. And you all deserve it!

I wanted to give you all a peak behind the curtain of what goes on in the summer in IT. I find it really funny when people are shocked that I don't get any time off in the summer; I will always respond with, "It's the 2nd busiest time of year for IT." Summer is our chance to do things that we just cannot do while there are staff and students here.

There are a lot of things that we prep so that you can have the most successful year possible. From updates to cleaning up cables in carts, we want to make sure that the stuff you don't have to think about, stays that way. A couple of examples are:

1. We physically clean ALL the devices we can get our hands on (chromebooks & iPads). You all know how neat and tidy kids are, so this may come as a shock, but let me assure you, it's VERY needed. 😂
2. We run updates on all student devices. We tend to hold off on major updates during the school year in case the update breaks something. So we run updates over the summer to test that everything is working like it should.
3. We replace old student devices with new ones. Yeah, tech gets old and gets to the point we have to replace it. This year we bought about 4,500 chromebooks and about 5,000 iPads for the whole district. We have to go weed out all the old devices, set them up, trade them out, update our inventories, and repeat.
4. We cleanup your charging cables in carts. This year we are going to have to change out a lot of cables because the new iPads have a new charger. So we will go in, cable manage everything, get it looking nice, so the kids can come destroy it after a few months.
5. Send off broken devices to our repair team. They get somewhere in the neighborhood of 2,000+ devices in the summer to repair.
6. This year we are replacing the green tags on the chromebooks with a different one that will hopefully make it easier to keep track of since we can scan multiple at once, and we hope to put it where the kids can't get to it as easily. RFID tags. super cool.
7. Do maintenance on printers and other tech that could need it.
8. We aim to replace a number of Windows devices that will take the latest updates.

And we have a number of other things that we do, but these are the main ones that we are focused on this summer. 

But don't feel too bad for us, a lot of us techs actually enjoy the summer jobs. It's a big change from what we do in the school year, many will put on some music, podcast, audiobook and get to work. It's very mindless work that you do a couple hundred times, it's nice to be able to put on some headphones and get to work.
