I want to take just a second and talk about cart timers. I've gotten questions about why we have them and how to use them.

## Why we have them
why is a pretty simple answer, **safety**. 

During covid we had an incident at Union Middle School. Some chromebooks were constantly charging, got too hot and caught fire. There was a decent amount of damage done to the school.

After the investigation was done, it was decided by the fire marshal that we have to keep all devices from charging too long. And that's where our timers were born.
