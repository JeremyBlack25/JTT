Signing into a browser saves you from seeing things you don't want to see.

#### Too Long Didn't Read; TLDR

Youtube was blocked for one day last week. This was due to inappropriate pictures in ads that was presented to the students. 
How to prevent: Be sure to log into your browser with your **csddocs.org** account.

---


Last week, there was an incident involving Youtube and students. A teacher was presenting on the projector and had some very inappropriate images come up on the advertisements to the right of the video. The entire class saw it and it sparked a big push from the district to find the root of it.

After the investigation, they found that the teacher hadn't logged into youtube or their browser. That caused youtube to show generic ads and not the restricted ones that we requested Google show us. We also believe that those ads were hijacked and have been fixed by Google, but we can't guarantee that. The best coarse of action is to make sure you are logged into your browser so our Google specific filter is active.

# Logging into your browser
Chrome and Firefox are the 2 supported broswers of the district.

Thank you, and have a happy week!
