Signing into a browser saves you from seeing things you don't want to see.

#### Too Long Didn't Read: TL;DR

Youtube was blocked for one day last week. This was due to inappropriate pictures in ads that were presented to the students. 
How to prevent: Be sure to log into your browser with your **csddocs.org** account.

---


Last week, there was an incident involving Youtube and students. While a teacher was projecting a video and had some very inappropriate images come up on the advertisements to the right of the video. The entire class saw it and it sparked a big push from the district to find the root of it.

After the investigation, they found that the teacher hadn't logged into youtube or their browser. That caused youtube to show generic ads and not the restricted ones that we requested Google show us. We also believe that those ads were hijacked and have been fixed by Google, but we can't guarantee that. The best course of action is to make sure you are logged into your browser so our Google specific filter is active.

If you log into you browser with your @csddocs.org email then it will automatically sign into youtube with that account.
# Logging into your browser
Chrome and Firefox are the 2 supported browsers of the district. I know most people use Chrome, so that's the instructions I'll include, let me know if you have another browser you have issues with.

**There are lots of nuances when it comes to adding your account, so PLEASE reach out if you have issues.**

First thing to look at is the top right of your browser window, click on your profile picture or the little person in a circle.

![[Screenshot 2026-02-17 at 9.33.19 AM.png]]

If it tells you "Sign in to Chrome" you want to click on that. it'll ask for an email; you want to put in your @csddocs.org email account. Then you put in your same password for all your district accounts.
You can click through everything, and then it'll come to a screen like this...
![[Screenshot 2026-02-17 at 9.36.36 AM.png]]

You want to make sure that you select "No, continue to use for school in this profile". Otherwise, you'll lose your browsing history, bookmarks, and saved passwords.

## Benefits to signing in
1. All your bookmarks and data are saved so you can easily log into any computer with the same account and have all your data there
2. You are more protected with our more aggressive internet filtering on @csddocs.org.
3. If you create another profile for your personal data, you can keep everything separated way easier.


As always if you need any help with your technology or have any questions, I'm happy to help! Be sure to submit a ticket through one of the official methods: email help@canyonsdistrict.org or by logging into [tdx.canyonsdistrict.org](http://tdx.canyonsdistrict.org).

Thank you, and have a happy week!
