# Copyright
This site is functions as a nod to the nostalgia of Disneyland and it's movies. Claiming itself under the Fair Use section 107 of Copyright laws. 
techno mumbo jumbo aside, it sits in the "gray area" of Copyright. Since they don't charge anything for access to the content and list Disney as the copyright owners and even push consumers to buy licensed merchendise from Disney, they give themselves a bit of grace if legal actions come up. Likely, Disney would issue a sease and disist if the website got too big for it's britches before full legal actions.
